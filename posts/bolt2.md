# Lightning Bolt and Its Implications
What is a <a href="https://scryfall.com/card/m11/149/lightning-bolt"> Bolt</a>?

Magic the Gathering^TM^ has a long extensive history. This article will assume some familiarities with terms. For example: A 1 mana 1/1 will refer to a creature that has a mana value of 1, with power and toughness equal to 1 respectively.
